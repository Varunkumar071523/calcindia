What is a Fixed Deposit?

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is one of the safest investment options in India. Banks and NBFCs offer fixed interest rates for a specified period.

How FD Maturity is Calculated

FD maturity amount depends on:

  • Principal Amount
  • Interest Rate
  • Investment Tenure
  • Compounding Frequency

Benefits of Fixed Deposits

  • Guaranteed Returns
  • Low Risk Investment
  • Flexible Tenure
  • Loan Against FD Facility
