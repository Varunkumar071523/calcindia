Income Tax in India

Income tax is charged on earnings from salary, business, investments and other sources. Choosing the correct tax regime can help reduce your tax burden.

New Tax Regime

The New Tax Regime offers lower tax rates but fewer deductions. It is suitable for taxpayers who do not claim significant exemptions.

Old Tax Regime

The Old Tax Regime allows deductions under sections such as 80C, 80D and HRA. It can be beneficial for taxpayers with large deductions.

How to Save Tax Legally

  • Invest under Section 80C
  • Health Insurance under 80D
  • NPS Contributions
  • Home Loan Benefits
  • Education Loan Benefits
